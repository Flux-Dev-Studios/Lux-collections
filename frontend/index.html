BEFORE YOU SET OFF

A few things
worth knowing.

A considered experience starts with knowing what to expect. Here is how your Lux journey works.

Start your journey →
When is my reservation confirmed?

Once you send a request, our team reviews the dates and vehicle. Your reservation is confirmed only when the team updates its status and you receive confirmation.

When will I be asked to pay?

Payment becomes available after your reservation is confirmed. You can pay the remaining balance in full, or choose a 50% payment or 20% deposit through secure Stripe Checkout.

What happens after payment?

Stripe securely processes your payment. We show a success message only after our server verifies the completed payment, and your reservation history reflects the amount received.

Can I change my dates or pick-up point?

Review your dates and pick-up location carefully before sending a request. For changes after submission, contact the Lux team through your reservation so they can review availability.
